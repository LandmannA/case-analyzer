Salesforce Case Analyzer

An AI tool that reads a pile of support cases like a Revenue Operations analyst would — classifying every case, spotting emerging trends, and writing the knowledge article that would prevent the most repeat tickets.

The Business Problem

A Head of Customer Service with thousands of open tickets and no dedicated analyst can't easily answer the questions that actually matter: what are customers contacting us about, what's trending up this month, and where should we invest in a knowledge article to stop repeat tickets before they happen? Today that analysis is manual, slow, and usually skipped.

What It Does

1
Load
CSV export, or one-click demo data
2
Classify
Category, cause, sentiment, urgency
3
Surface
Trends & a written summary
4
Suggest
Which article to write next
5
Track
Case volume, before & after

Why It's Worth Using

No analyst required
Classifies hundreds of cases in seconds — work that would otherwise take a person days to triage by hand.
Catches issues early
Flags a new or accelerating problem the month it appears, not the month a customer escalates it.
Turns insight into action
Doesn't stop at a chart — recommends the exact knowledge article to write next.
Proves its own impact
Tracks case volume for a topic before and after its article is published, so the payoff is measurable.

The Dashboard

Live output from the demo dataset: 178 synthetic cases classified, category and sentiment breakdowns, volume trend over time.

Where the Value Shows Up

Month-by-month topic tracking flags new and accelerating issues automatically, and measures the impact of articles already published — this run shows a published article correlating with a 72% drop in related case volume.
